Simple CRM

Every customer, job, estimate and invoice — from quote to paid.

An Android app for contractors, HVAC techs, electricians and plumbers. It is built around the customer rather than a list of jobs: open someone up and you see what they owe, what is booked in, every project you have run for them and the whole history.

Everything is stored on the phone and works with no signal. There is no account to create and nothing to sign into.

What it does

  • Customers with addresses, gate codes, access notes and a full timeline
  • Projects made of jobs, with photos, notes and a checklist
  • Estimates from your price book that become scheduled work in one tap
  • Invoices with tax and terms applied, and part payments tracked properly
  • Costs logged against a project, so you see margin and not just price
  • A day view of what is booked and where

Testing

Simple CRM is in closed testing on Google Play. If you have an invite, accept it with the same Google account you use on your phone, then install from the Play Store.

Your data is on your device only — there is no cloud backup yet. Please do not use this build as the only record of anything you cannot afford to lose.

Privacy
